Threads That Carry Generations

Every Kente Collective piece begins with an artisan — a weaver in Bonwire, a dyer in Abeokuta, a beader in Nairobi. We partner directly with 47 makers across West and East Africa, paying fair wages and sharing their stories with every garment.

We believe fashion should honour its makers. That's why each tag carries the name of the artisan who created your piece.
