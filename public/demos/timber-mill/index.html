The Atelier

Wood has a
memory of its own

Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the grain, knots, and character of its tree — no two are ever alike. That specificity is not an imperfection; it is the point.

TimberMill was founded in 2009 by master craftsman Emeka Osei, who trained in traditional cabinetmaking before establishing his studio in Lagos. Today we work with private clients, architects, and interior designers across West Africa and the UK.

We work exclusively in solid hardwood — American white oak, black walnut, iroko, and afromosia. No veneers, no MDF, no shortcuts.

How It Works

From brief to
finished piece

Every commission follows the same four-stage process — deliberate, unhurried, and fully transparent at each step.

01
Consultation

We begin with a conversation — in-person at the workshop or over video. We discuss the space, intended use, timber preferences, and any reference imagery you have in mind.

02
Design & Quotation

We produce scaled drawings and a detailed quotation within five working days. Revisions are welcome at this stage — there is no cost until you are satisfied with the design.

03
Making

Lead time is typically six to eight weeks. You are welcome to visit the workshop mid-build. We send photographic updates at each major stage — milling, joinery, and finishing.

04
Delivery & Fitting

We deliver and install the piece ourselves — no couriers. Built-in furniture includes on-site finishing and a one-year structural warranty as standard.
