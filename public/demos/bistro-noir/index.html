Executive Chef

Adaeze Okonkwo

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and shaped in the kitchens of Dakar and Lyon, Chef Adaeze returned home to redefine what fine dining means in Lagos. Her philosophy is simple — honour the ingredient, respect the origin, surprise the palate.

"Every plate tells two stories: the technique of France, and the heartbeat of West Africa."
