— 01 / PHILOSOPHY

We design buildings that belong to their place — rooted in climate, culture, and the quiet rhythms of daily life.

Cityscape was founded on a simple belief: that architecture is not the imposition of form, but a careful conversation between site, material, and the people who inhabit the space.

— 02 / STUDIO

A small studio with a long view.

Founded by architects Chioma Eze and Klaus Bauer, Cityscape operates as an intimate practice of fourteen designers, theorists, and craftspeople. We take on a deliberately limited number of projects each year — because detail cannot be rushed.

Our work has been featured in Dezeen, ArchDaily, and the Venice Biennale of Architecture 2023.
